\section{A small revision example}
Let \(f:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R\) be differentiable at \(x\).
For \(x\in\mathbb R\), consider the increment
\[
  f(x+h)-f(x).
\]

The first-order approximation is
\[
  f(x+h)=f(x)+f'(x)h+o(h),\qquad h\to0.
\]

This example contains two independent revision proposals.
